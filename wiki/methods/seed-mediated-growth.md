# Seed-Mediated Growth

Seed-mediated growth uses small Au seeds, CTAB and a mild reducing agent to grow larger or anisotropic particles [L5, p.13]. The notes list seeds around 3–5 nm and CTAB (cetyltrimethylammonium bromide) [L5, p.13].

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Seeds["Au seeds (3–5 nm)"] --> CTAB["CTAB shell"]
  CTAB --> Growth["Precursor + mild reducer"]
  Growth --> Shape["Anisotropic Au particle"]
  Shape --> Photo["Photothermal treatment"]
```
*Takeaway: preformed seeds separate nucleation from shape-directed growth.*
