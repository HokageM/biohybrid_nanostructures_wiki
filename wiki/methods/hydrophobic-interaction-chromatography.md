# Hydrophobic Interaction Chromatography

Hydrophobic interaction chromatography separates proteins by hydrophobic surface regions; it is listed in the lecture as a way to separate proteins according to hydrophobic regions [L1, p.6].

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  HighSalt["High-salt sample"] --> Bind["Hydrophobic patches bind resin"]
  Bind --> Gradient["Decrease salt"]
  Gradient --> Elute["Proteins elute by hydrophobicity"]
```
*Takeaway: hydrophobic chromatography exploits the same water-release physics as protein folding.*
