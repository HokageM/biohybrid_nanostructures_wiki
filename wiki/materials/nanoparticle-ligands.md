# Nanoparticle Ligands

Nanoparticle ligands bind the surface and form a [[ligand-shell]] that controls stability, solubility, accessibility, [[surface-charge]] and interparticle interactions [L5, pp.8,13–14]. A ligand has a head group, an intermolecular shell region and terminal groups [L5, p.14].

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Head["Head group binds NP"] --> Spacer["Intermolecular region"]
  Spacer --> Tail["Terminal group"]
  Tail --> Solvent["Solubility / compatibility"]
```
*Takeaway: ligand architecture translates core chemistry into solution behaviour.*

Examples include citrate, CTAB, thiols, PEG, alkyl chains and polymers [L5, p.14]. Hydrophobic ligands also connect ligand shells to [[hydrophobic-effect]] and [[van-der-waals-interactions]] during assembly [L11, pp.1?2].
