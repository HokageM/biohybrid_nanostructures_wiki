# Zeta Potential

Zeta potential is the electrical potential at the [[debye-length-slipping-plane|slipping plane]] separating mobile liquid from liquid attached to a particle surface [L6, p.21]. It indicates colloid stability [L6, p.21].

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Surface["Charged particle surface"] --> Stern["Attached fluid"]
  Stern --> Slip["Slipping plane: ζ"]
  Slip --> Bulk["Mobile bulk fluid"]
```
*Takeaway: ζ potential reports the effective potential during particle motion.*

Rule of thumb from the notes: $\zeta<-30$ mV or $\zeta>+30$ mV means stable dispersion; $\zeta\approx0$ mV near the [[isoelectric-point]] lowers stability [L6, p.21]. It depends on [[surface-charge]], pH, ionic strength, additives and ligands [L6, p.21].
