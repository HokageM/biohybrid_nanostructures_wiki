# Enzyme Production in E. coli

The lectures use *E. coli* production as the practical route from a gene to a protein or enzyme for research and industry. Applications include enzymes for molecular biology, vaccines and antibodies [L1, p.3], and designed enzymes that require expression and screening [L2, p.7].

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  Design["Gene / enzyme design"] --> Plasmid["Plasmid"]
  Plasmid --> Ecoli["E. coli expression"]
  Ecoli --> Purify["Protein purification"]
  Purify --> Test["Activity / identity test"]
```
*Takeaway: biological production makes engineered enzymes experimentally accessible.*

## Limits
E. coli is cheap and fast but may misfold eukaryotic proteins and cannot provide all eukaryotic post-translational modifications [L1, p.5].
