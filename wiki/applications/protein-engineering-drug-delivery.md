The notes do not present a full drug-delivery lecture, but they do mention protein cages as containers with interiors for encapsulation and exteriors for carrier groups [L2, p.9], and D-peptides as pharmaceutically interesting because of stability, protease resistance, reduced immunogenicity and enhanced bioavailability/activity [L1, p.4].
